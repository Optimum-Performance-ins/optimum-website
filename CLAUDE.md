# Optimum Performance — Brand Guidelines

## Company
- **Name**: Optimum Performance
- **Tagline**: "It's not only business, It's a business culture"
- **Industry**: Business consultancy (strategy, operations, HR, training, digital transformation)

## Logo
- Shield/crest with "OP" monogram inside a circle
- File: `static/OP_original.png`
- Multiple variants exist (light/dark backgrounds) in `optimum performance all.ai`

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Garamond (EB Garamond via Google Fonts)
- **Weights**: Regular (400), Italic, Bold (700)
- Used for all headings and body text

## Color Palette
| Color     | Hex       | Usage                          |
|-----------|-----------|--------------------------------|
| Deep Navy | `#171025` | Primary background, base color |
| Dark Teal | `#203c49` | Secondary background           |
| Blue      | `#3c7696` | Accents, highlights, links     |

### Gradient Flow (used across sections)
The website uses a continuous gradient that flows between these three colors. Each section's top matches the previous section's bottom — no hard color jumps.

## Brand Tone
- Professional, refined, corporate
- Elegant and minimal (no flashy animations)
- Confident but not aggressive
- Emphasis on partnership, trust, and measurable results

## Website Structure (single-page scroll)
1. Hero (logo + tagline)
2. Who We Are
3. Vision & Mission
4. Core Values (Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Client Focus, Teamwork, Results-Driven)
5. Our Services (Strategy, Business Dev, Operational Excellence, HR & Talent, Training, Digital Transformation)
6. Competitive Advantage
7. Trusted By (client logos)
8. CEO Message
9. Footer (contact, links)

## Key Files
- `index.html` — Single-page website
- `styles.css` — All styling, responsive design, animations
- `script.js` — Smooth scroll, sticky nav, fade-in animations, mobile menu
- `static/OP_original.png` — Logo
- `OP-Profile.pdf` — Full company profile document
- `Component 1.png` — Profile overview image
- `optimum performance all.ai` — Brand assets (Adobe Illustrator)
